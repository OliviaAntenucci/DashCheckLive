DashCheckLive — Product Requirements Document
=============================================
Status: Hackathon prototype (v0.1)    Date: 2026-11-07

1. PROBLEM
----------
During peak periods restaurant staff juggle orders from multiple sources while
packing food, checking items and coordinating pickups. This takes significant
labour and constant communication, and leads to missing items, mixed-up orders
and incorrect handoffs. Missing items cost the restaurant refunds and ratings,
cost DoorDash support contacts and redeliveries, and cost customers their meal
(or, for allergy notes, their safety).

Focus area: PICKUP ERRORS: fewer missing items and wrong handoffs.

2. SOLUTION
-----------
DashCheckLive is a feature DoorDash provides inside the merchant app at the final
packing step. Restaurant staff:
  a) get a packing checklist built from the order AND the customer's free-text
     note (personal data removed before any AI sees it),
  b) lay items out in a check zone where a local camera model counts them and
     flags what looks missing,
  c) confirm (or override with a reason) and seal the order,
  d) hand off only to the assigned Dasher, who must scan a one-time signed QR.
Every step is recorded in a tamper-evident log.

AI assists; people decide. The model never seals an order.

3. USERS
--------
  Primary   Restaurant packer / expo staff (merchant_staff role). Busy, gloved
            hands, needs glanceable status and one-tap actions.
  Secondary Dasher (dasher role). Needs a fast, unambiguous pickup.
  Tertiary  Restaurant owner / DoorDash support. Needs evidence when a customer
            reports a missing item.

4. USER STORIES
---------------
  US1  As a packer, I see exactly which items, extras and utensils go in the bag,
       including those buried in the customer note ("extra ranch pls").
  US2  As a packer, I'm warned when an item I laid out appears missing, before
       the bag is closed.
  US3  As a packer, I see allergy flags and prep requests the camera can't check,
       and must acknowledge them.
  US4  As a packer, I can override the camera with a reason when it's wrong.
  US5  As a Dasher, I can only pick up orders assigned to me, and only once.
  US6  As an owner/support agent, I can see who sealed an order, whether vision
       agreed, any overrides, and who picked it up, and trust it wasn't edited.
  US7  As a customer, my phone number and address aren't shown on kitchen screens
       or sent to AI services.

5. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------
  FR1  Staff and Dasher authenticate; each API endpoint is restricted to one role.
  FR2  Customer notes are scrubbed (phone, email, address, card, URL, customer
       name) before display or any external call. Redaction counts are shown.
  FR3  Scrubbed note is parsed into: extras, exclusions, prep instructions,
       allergy flags. LLM via OpenRouter; deterministic rules fallback.
  FR4  LLM output is validated against an allow-list; notes cannot remove paid
       items; parser used is recorded.
  FR5  Camera frames (~2 fps) or an uploaded photo are processed locally by an
       open-vocabulary detector using labels derived from the order.
  FR6  Counts are smoothed (median of last 5 frames); boxes >= 0.35 confidence
       count; 0.20–0.35 produce "check manually".
  FR7  Checklist rows: ok / missing / check manually / unexpected (excluded item
       present).
  FR8  Seal requires: signed-in staff, explicit confirmation, prep/allergy
       acknowledgement when present, and a reason for every non-ok row.
       Enforced server-side.
  FR9  Seal issues an HMAC-signed, expiring, single-use pickup token (QR) bound
       to order + assigned Dasher.
  FR10 Pickup rejects: bad signature, expired, wrong Dasher, replay, stale token.
  FR11 All significant events are appended to a hash-chained log; the UI shows
       chain validity. No PII in the log.
  FR12 Photo upload works when the webcam is unavailable; the app works (with
       overrides) when the detector is not installed.

6. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
------------------------------
  NFR1 Privacy: images never leave the device; only scrubbed text goes to the LLM.
  NFR2 Latency: < 1 s per frame on a laptop CPU (yolov8s-worldv2, 640 px).
  NFR3 Reliability: no single AI failure blocks an order; failures fail safe
       (never auto-approve).
  NFR4 Security headers (CSP, nosniff, frame-ancestors none); secrets in .env.
  NFR5 Usability: status readable at arm's length; red/amber/green semantics.

7. SUCCESS METRICS
------------------
  Demo:   detect a missing sauce cup live; reject wrong Dasher and replay;
          show intact hash chain; injection note has no effect.
  Product (pilot hypotheses, not measured here):
    - Missing-item reports per 1,000 orders  ↓ 30%
    - Wrong-handoff incidents                 ↓ 90%
    - Added pack time per order               < 10 s median
    - Override rate (proxy for model quality) tracked per item label

8. OUT OF SCOPE
---------------
  Real DoorDash APIs / POS integration; detecting items inside closed bags;
  verifying allergens or ingredients; production auth (SSO/MFA); multi-store.
  See SCOPE.md.

9. RISKS
--------
  - Detector misses small/occluded items → "check manually" + human seal.
  - Staff over-trust green checks → copy says "helper, not proof"; prep and
    allergies always require explicit acknowledgement.
  - Prompt injection via notes → allow-list output, no model authority.
  - Added friction at rush hour → only flags exceptions; one-tap seal when all ok.

10. TECH
--------
  Backend:  Python, FastAPI, ultralytics YOLO-World (local), OpenRouter
            (openai/gpt-4o-mini, JSON mode), HMAC-SHA256, PBKDF2, SHA-256 chain.
  Frontend: single HTML page, vanilla JS, getUserMedia + canvas overlay.
