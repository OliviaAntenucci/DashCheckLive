DashCheckLive

Make sure every order is complete before it leaves your restaurant. The camera checks the items against the order, so fewer meals go out missing something.

  1. Sign in with your staff ID and PIN.
  2. Pick an order and lay its items in front of the camera. Each item is checked off as it's counted.
  3. Finalize the order: tick “I'm done” and seal it.
  4. Hand it off. The Dasher scans the pickup code and takes the order.

Dashers: sign in with your Dasher ID to confirm a pickup.
